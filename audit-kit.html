📋 Quick-Reference Cold-Chain & Data Anonymization SOP Checklist

Follow these four standard operating checks before uploading clinic records into Twevix Care™:

1. Strip All Direct Patient Identifiers:
Remove patient names, national IDs, phone numbers, and street addresses. Use anonymized visit codes (e.g., VST-1042) only.
2. Standardize Cold-Chain Logging Intervals:
Record vaccine & reagent refrigerator temperatures at minimum every 4 hours (06:00, 10:00, 14:00, 18:00, 22:00) in Celsius.
3. Flag Excursion Thresholds (<2.0°C or >8.0°C):
Any sustained reading outside 2.0°C – 8.0°C triggers an IsolationForest anomaly alert and requires quarantine inspection.
4. Mint Cryptographic Audit Receipts:
Export your TVX-CARE- SHA-256 receipt after each monthly audit and verify authenticity anytime on TWEVIX PULSE™.
